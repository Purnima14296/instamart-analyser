## Phase 4 Evaluation

## Purpose

Validate that the system can translate validated insights into credible product opportunity areas and experimentable hypotheses.

## What To Test

- each opportunity maps back to one or more validated insights
- recommendations do not overclaim causality
- opportunity framing separates user problem, product intervention, and expected business effect
- hypotheses are specific enough to test
- prioritization logic reflects business value and feasibility

## Validation Methods

- stakeholder review with growth, product, and research
- traceability check from recommendation to insight to raw evidence
- hypothesis quality review using a standard experiment-design rubric
- challenge session to identify weak assumptions and confounders

## Exit Criteria

- every opportunity area is evidence-backed
- hypotheses are specific, testable, and scoped
- priority recommendations are understandable to non-authors
- assumptions and unknowns are documented
- stakeholders agree the output is ready for experiment planning

## Failure Signals

- recommendations are generic and not tied to evidence
- insights are misused as proof of causality
- hypotheses are too broad to test
- prioritization depends on opinion rather than documented logic
