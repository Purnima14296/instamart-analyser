## Phase 0 Evaluation

## Purpose

Validate that the team has framed the right problem, defined a usable taxonomy, and agreed on what the AI agent must produce.

## What To Test

- the business objective is explicit and measurable
- the analysis questions map directly to the problem statement
- the taxonomy covers the expected range of barriers, triggers, and missions
- the output schema is specific enough for downstream aggregation
- insight-quality criteria are documented

## Validation Methods

- stakeholder review with product and growth
- manual walkthrough of 20 to 30 sample feedback items against the taxonomy
- schema review to confirm no critical business fields are missing
- contradiction review to ensure taxonomy labels do not overlap confusingly

## Exit Criteria

- business objective is approved
- taxonomy v1 is documented and usable
- output schema is finalized for implementation
- priority source list is agreed
- insight rubric is written and accepted

## Failure Signals

- team members interpret labels differently
- sample feedback cannot be classified cleanly
- important business questions do not map to available fields
- downstream phases would require frequent schema rework
