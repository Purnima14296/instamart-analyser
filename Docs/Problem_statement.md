## Problem Statement

Selected platform: Swiggy Instamart.

Assume you are a Product Manager on the Growth Team at Swiggy Instamart.

## Business Context

Swiggy Instamart has become a regular part of many users' weekly and monthly shopping routines. Many customers repeatedly use the platform to purchase groceries, snacks and beverages, household essentials, and other frequently needed items.

This repeat behavior is valuable because it drives retention and habit formation. However, it also creates a growth challenge for Instamart: users often come to the app with a narrow mission, buy the same products from the same categories, and leave without exploring the wider assortment available on the platform.

As a result, category penetration remains limited for a large share of Monthly Active Customers. Even when the platform offers relevant adjacent categories such as personal care, pet supplies, baby products, or home needs, many users do not discover or adopt them.

## Core Problem

Shopping behavior on quick-commerce platforms becomes highly repetitive over time. Users tend to stick to familiar purchase patterns and rarely expand into new categories.

This limits:

- customer lifetime value
- cross-category adoption
- basket expansion opportunities
- the platform's ability to build broader shopping habits

The company wants to understand why this happens and identify ways to help users confidently discover and purchase from new categories.

## Objective

Increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers who purchase from at least one new category every month.

Examples:

- A user who usually buys groceries starts buying pet supplies.
- A user who usually buys snacks starts buying personal care products.
- A user who usually buys household essentials starts buying baby products.

## What You Need To Solve

Before proposing product ideas or interventions, you must first build a strong understanding of the user problem.

Specifically, you need to answer:

- Why do users repeatedly buy from the same categories?
- What prevents them from exploring new categories?
- How do users discover products today?
- What role do habits, trust, urgency, and convenience play in category exploration?
- What information or reassurance do users need before trying a new category?
- What frustrations or unmet needs appear consistently across user feedback?
- Which user segments are more open to experimentation than others?

## Part 1: Build an AI-Powered Discovery Engine

Before recommending solutions, build an AI-powered system that can analyze user feedback at scale and surface high-quality insights about category discovery behavior.

You may use any AI-native stack of your choice, including:

- Claude
- GPTs
- Agents
- Workflows
- RAG systems
- n8n
- Zapier
- Perplexity
- any equivalent tooling

## Data Sources To Analyze

Your discovery engine should be able to gather and analyze signals from sources such as:

- App Store reviews
- Play Store reviews
- Reddit discussions
- community forums
- social media conversations
- product reviews
- broader quick-commerce discussions

## Expected Output From The Discovery Engine

Your system should help generate evidence-backed answers to questions such as:

- Why do users stay within the same categories?
- What triggers exploration into a new category?
- What blocks trial for adjacent categories?
- How much of the behavior is driven by habit versus poor discovery?
- What role do trust, pricing, assortment, and product information play?
- Which themes appear repeatedly across different feedback channels?
- Which user segments, missions, or occasions are most likely to lead to cross-category expansion?

## What You Should Demonstrate

Show clearly:

- how your workflow gathers and processes data
- how the system organizes and tags themes
- how insights are generated from raw feedback
- how you validate that the insights are reliable, relevant, and not just anecdotal

## Final Goal

Use the discovery engine to build a well-grounded understanding of the real user barriers and opportunities behind category expansion. The final outcome should create a strong foundation for later product recommendations that can increase new-category adoption among Monthly Active Customers.
